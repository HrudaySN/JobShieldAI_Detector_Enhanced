Our AI model analyzes the job description text and/or uploaded file for suspicious keywords, unrealistic salary claims, company authenticity signals, contact details, and grammar quality to predict how likely a posting is to be fake.

We flag common patterns such as upfront payment/deposit requests, work-from-home data entry scams, fake recruiter chat-only interviews, unrealistic pay-for-effort offers, and impersonation of well-known companies.

This project currently uses a small, explainable baseline trained on the included labelled starter dataset. Its score is a risk indicator, not a guarantee; every result shows the matched signals so you can make the final call and verify the company independently.
